PROJECT 001 / INTELLIGENT COMPUTING

TinyBrain

Rethinking how intelligent systems use computing resources.

TinyBrain is an experimental initiative exploring efficient local AI execution, memory management and resource-aware processing.

Its long-term direction is to investigate ways to make useful AI systems more accessible on hardware with limited resources.

Areas of exploration:

  • Lightweight AI model execution
  • Memory and resource management
  • Model optimization and quantization
  • Experimental distributed computing

Status: Experimental development.

PROJECT 002 / LOCAL AI ASSISTANT

Rias AI

A more independent approach to intelligent assistance.

Rias AI is an experimental assistant project exploring local AI processing, offline-first experiences and accessible human-computer interaction.

The project investigates how intelligent assistants can operate closer to the user, with greater independence from remote AI services.

Areas of exploration:

  • Locally operated language models
  • Offline-capable experiences
  • Conversational interfaces
  • Privacy-conscious system design

Status: Experimental development.
