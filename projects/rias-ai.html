SYNTRIX PROJECT / 002

Intelligence that
works closer to you.

Rias AI is an experimental assistant initiative exploring local artificial intelligence, offline-capable experiences and more accessible human-computer interaction.

Discover Rias AI

01 / THE IDEA

A more independent AI experience.

Many AI assistants depend on remote infrastructure and constant internet connectivity. Rias AI explores an alternative direction: bringing more of the intelligence and processing closer to the user.

The project investigates local language models, offline-capable interactions and interfaces designed to make AI easier to use.

02 / RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

What we're exploring

Local AI Processing

Exploring language models that can run directly on supported user devices.

Offline Experiences

Investigating how useful assistant features can remain available with limited connectivity.

Human Interaction

Exploring intuitive interfaces, conversational experiences and accessible AI design.

03 / OUR APPROACH

Designed around people.

Rias AI explores technology that is approachable, useful and conscious of the user's resources. The goal is to investigate ways to make AI feel less dependent on powerful remote infrastructure.

Privacy-conscious design and local processing are important research considerations, not guarantees of complete privacy or security.

04 / THE VISION

Useful intelligence, more accessible.

Rias AI is part of Syntrix Dynamics Technologies' broader exploration of independent computing and human-centred artificial intelligence.

The project remains under experimental development. Its features, architecture and capabilities continue to evolve through testing and research.
